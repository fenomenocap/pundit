# Offline fixture-specific totals development contract

This contract is recorded before candidate fitting or hyperparameter selection. The existing 760 joined Premier League results from 2024–26 and the already inspected current-season results are development/exposed data. Replaying them cannot create an independent blind test. Production selection, constants, rating artifacts, ledger and promotion gates are unchanged.

## Candidate family and numerical repairs

The candidate retains time-decayed attack/defence goal rates, so total expected goals can differ by fixture. It uses a rho parameterization that guarantees nonnegative Dixon–Coles correction factors for every ordered pairing of fitted clubs, including pairings absent from training. Dated Elo fallback clubs still require independent forecast validation. A bounded L-BFGS optimizer with an analytic gradient and Armijo line search reports its actual gradient norm and stopping reason. A small parameterization margin protects floating-point boundaries; it is a numerical safeguard, not a predictive acceptance threshold.

The shipped research fitter remains available as the baseline. No response or live model uses this candidate. Synthetic finite-difference checks, all-pair tests and chronological negative controls precede empirical runs. Solver budget and candidate-family choices are fixed before observing new candidate holdout losses.

## Chronological development and empirical reporting

Outer development evaluation uses non-overlapping weekly holdouts, an expanding earlier training window, a 24-hour result-availability lag, and Elo dated at least one day before kickoff. If hyperparameter selection is needed, its candidates and criterion are declared before execution and all selection folds must end inside that outer training window. Fitted parameters, club universe, priors and hyperparameter selection never see that outer holdout. The already exposed outer results remain development diagnostics even with correct chronological splitting.

Report 1X2 Brier/log loss, over-2.5 Brier/log loss, BTTS Brier/log loss, exact-score log loss, fixed reliability buckets, total-goal variation and prediction-versus-outcome summaries. Use paired UTC-week uncertainty; identify interval coverage, resample count and seed. Keep every attempted origin and fixture in coverage/convergence denominators, including rejected fits/grids. Compare common valid rows explicitly rather than silently improving metrics by dropping failures. A nonconverged fit is not an accepted forecast.

## Acceptance and prospective reserve

Existing gates are retained; no favorable retrospective metric waives them. This experiment cannot recommend activation because it has no untouched post-development reserve. Freeze candidate code, options, training data and parameter hashes before collecting prospective seals. Outcomes after that freeze supply independent evidence. Additional earlier historical seasons may expand training/development but do not make the already observed 2024–26 results blind. Any future promotion requires multi-market evidence, adequate uncertainty and a separate reviewed decision.

## Predeclared selection configuration

Before candidate outer losses are computed, the inner grid is fixed to time decay xi `{0.002, 0.0065}` and Elo prior strength `{8, 24}`. These bracket a longer-memory specification and the existing decay, and the existing and stronger low-sample prior. The solver budget is 1,000 iterations, history ten, Armijo constant 0.0001 and at most forty backtracking evaluations. Convergence requires Euclidean gradient norm at most 0.000001. No empirical score changes these numerical settings.

At an outer origin, up to three successive 28-day inner validation blocks end before its 24-hour availability cutoff. Each inner training window contains at least forty earlier fixtures and applies the same lag. Selection minimizes mean `(1X2 Brier / 2 + over-2.5 Brier + BTTS Brier) / 3`, weighted by fixture count. This is a declared sum of proper probability scores, with multiclass Brier scaled to its range; it does not target one observed match or cherry-pick a market. All attempted folds, convergence failures and invalid forecasts are recorded. An option with any unaccepted fold/forecast is ineligible. When early history provides no eligible inner fold, predeclared defaults xi=0.0065/prior=8 are used, labelled unselected. Ties use grid order. No option is selected from an outer result, the previous aggregate report or the full-corpus artifact parameters.

Reliability summaries use fixed probability buckets `[0,.2,.4,.6,.8,1]`; goal-rate summaries use boundaries `0,2,2.5,3,3.5,4,+infinity`. These are diagnostic bins, not promotion thresholds. Existing 2,000-draw paired UTC-week bootstrap seed 20260915 supplies p10–p90 (80% empirical) intervals. The candidate remains ineligible for production regardless of these exposed development results.

## Numerical precision repair and recent training refresh

The first preserved candidate run accepted only31 of69 origins under the strict gradient threshold. Many searches stopped or consumed their budget just above that tolerance, because subtracting large floating-point likelihood totals hid Armijo increments. Before considering predictive loss changes, the optimizer comparator was repaired to accumulate mathematically equivalent likelihood increments using `expm1`, `log1p` and prior quadratic differences. Synthetic equivalence, antisymmetry and a cancellation regression verify the comparator. The1e-6 gradient threshold,1,000-iteration budget, option grid, objective and splits remain unchanged; the original run is preserved. Nonsmooth maximum-bound failures remain genuine failures if they persist.

The final prospective candidate may additionally fit existing exposed official PL seals. Each accepted record must have a completed nonnegative integer result, a complete pre-kickoff seal and exact matching content-addressed rating artifact and snapshot timestamp. Public model rows serialize ratings to one decimal: the sealed value must exactly equal `Math.round(artifactRating * 10) / 10`. Offline training recovers full precision from that verified artifact; it does not tolerate arbitrary differences or infer an earlier rating date. The snapshot date must precede kickoff day. Missing, tampered, same-day, duplicate and unavailable records retain exclusion reasons. These are incomplete current-season training data, not independent validation. Their actual observation timestamp supplies result availability; the ledger's default `builtAt` and forecast timestamp do not. They refresh fitted parameters only; the hyperparameters remain the preceding historical inner selection, rather than being selected on these recent results.

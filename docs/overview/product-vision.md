# Product Vision

Pundit's premise: the most useful thing you can do with a prediction model isn't stare at a probability table, it's ask it a question and get a straight answer.

Most tournament-prediction tools stop at a dashboard — win probabilities, expected goals, a bracket simulator. Pundit puts a conversational layer on top of that: name a matchup, and Pundit tells you what the numbers say, where the model and the market disagree, and what would have to be true for an upset — grounded in a real statistical model and live market pricing rather than an LLM guessing.

**Today**, that's a multi-turn chat backed by a Dixon-Coles Poisson model, full World Cup fixture history, live late-stage fixtures, and reference Stake/Kalshi/Polymarket prices — all read-only, informational, nothing to trade.

**Where this is designed to grow:** the same grounded-analysis approach extends naturally beyond a single tournament — to other sports, other markets, and richer ways of exploring a model's output than a single Q&A turn. Nothing beyond the current chat-first product is live yet; this section describes direction, not a shipped roadmap or a financial commitment.

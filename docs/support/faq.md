# FAQ

**Can I bet or trade on Pundit?**
No. Pundit doesn't run markets, hold funds, or offer any way to place a wager. It's an analysis tool — see the [Disclaimer](disclaimer.md).

**Which matches receive model-grounded chat answers?**
Scheduled or live semifinals, the 3rd-place match, and the championship final with confirmed teams. Other questions still work as tournament-grounded or clearly labelled general analysis.

**How current is the data?**
ESPN fixtures/standings and featured market odds refresh every 30 minutes; the local Elo/model refreshes hourly; Polymarket outright/group reference markets refresh every 6 hours. If a source is briefly unavailable, Pundit keeps the last-known-good data. The Fixtures page shows the ESPN cache timestamp and re-polls while matches are live.

**Where do the odds come from?**
Model probabilities come from Pundit's local Dixon-Coles model. Active fixture comparisons use its normalized Stake, Kalshi, and Polymarket 1X2 data. See [Data Sources](../how-it-works/data-sources.md).

**Does "home"/"away" mean anything for World Cup matches?**
Not in terms of home-field advantage — all 2026 World Cup matches are at neutral venues. The labels are positional only.

**Is there an API?**
Yes, all data shown in the app is available read-only with no authentication. See the [API Reference](../api-reference/overview.md).

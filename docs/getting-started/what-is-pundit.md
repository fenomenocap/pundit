# What is Pundit

Pundit is a chat-first analysis app for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Its live featured-match experience focuses on active semifinals and the championship final, combining Dixon-Coles probabilities with public 1X2 market prices and a plain-language Claude analysis.

It is **not** a betting or trading product. Pundit doesn't take positions, hold funds, or offer wagers. See the [Disclaimer](../support/disclaimer.md).

### Answer types

1. **Model-grounded match** — an ESPN-active semifinal or final with win/draw/win, totals, BTTS, scoreline probabilities, and any valid active Stake/Kalshi/Polymarket prices.
2. **Model-grounded tournament** — title analysis backed by the model's current team win probabilities.
3. **General analysis** — football help that is explicitly labelled as not grounded in Pundit's statistical model.

All World Cup matches are neutral-site fixtures. “Home” and “away” are positional labels only.

### What you can do

* Ask about the active semifinal or final suggested on the homepage
* Ask tournament-level and conversational follow-up questions
* Browse the full fixtures, results, and standings history
* Browse the companion model site and use the read-only API

Arsenal are dealing with some key injury concerns as we head towards the return of the Premier League next weekend.

It has been a difficult international break thus far for the Gunners, who have been hit by some high-profile setbacks and other scares involving important players.

Christos Tzolis and Kai Havertz are both now sidelined, while Martin Odegaard has been battling an issue of his own and Declan Rice withdrew from England duty right at the start of an extended three-week window.

A week before Tzolis' injury, fellow Arsenal forward Havertz had already suffered his own hamstring issue against the Netherlands while playing for Germany in the Nations League.

He too was an early withdrawal and also headed back to Arsenal, having only been named in Jurgen Klopp's split squad for two of Germany's four total games across the window.
